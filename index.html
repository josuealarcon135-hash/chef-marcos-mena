The Private Chef

CHEF MARCOS MENA

Rooted in the coastal beauty of Quepos, blending authentic Costa Rican soul with world-class gastronomic technique.

Born and raised with the rhythmic tides of the Central Pacific, Chef Marcos Mena cultivated a deep respect for line-caught seafood and tropical agriculture from an early age. Over the past 15 years, he has led high-end kitchens and cooked in the most prestigious private villas and yachts in Manuel Antonio.

His philosophy is simple: source only the freshest catch brought in daily at Marina Pez Vela, pair it with organic herbs and produce from local farmers, and execute every course with heartfelt warmth, theatrical fire, and uncompromising attention to detail.

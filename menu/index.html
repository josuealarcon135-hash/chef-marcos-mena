Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking your private chef in Costa Rica.

Does the pricing include groceries and ingredients?

Yes, completely. All packages and quotes include morning grocery sourcing of premium seafood and prime meats, fresh organic produce, villa travel, on-site live preparation, table service, and thorough kitchen sanitation.

How do you handle severe food allergies and special diets?

We accommodate gluten-free, vegetarian, vegan, dairy-free, and celiac diets at no extra charge. Simply specify dietary requirements during booking so we can sanitize utensils and procure safe ingredients.

How far in advance should we reserve?

We recommend booking 1 to 4 weeks in advance, particularly during high season (December through May). For last-minute inquiries, reach out on WhatsApp to check immediate calendar openings.

Which areas in Costa Rica do you service?

Our home base is Manuel Antonio and Quepos. We also regularly serve private villas in Dominical, Uvita, Esterillos, Jacó, Los Sueños Marina, Papagayo, Santa Teresa, and luxury estates throughout Costa Rica.

What happens if we go sportfishing in Marina Pez Vela?

We offer our signature Catch & Cook experience: you bring back your cleaned tuna, mahi-mahi, or snapper, and Chef Marcos crafts a multi-course culinary dinner highlighting your fresh catch with gourmet reductions and side dishes.

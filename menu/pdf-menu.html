FAQ & POLICIES

PREGUNTAS FRECUENTES & POLÍTICAS DE SERVICIO EN VILLAS

1. HOW DOES GROCERY SHOPPING WORK?

Chef Marcos personally sources all groceries from local organic farmers markets, artisan bakeries, and the Quepos marina docks daily. Only the freshest catch and prime produce are selected for your villa.

2. WHAT TIME DOES THE CHEF ARRIVE?

For breakfast, our culinary team arrives 60-90 minutes prior to your preferred serving time. For lunch and dinner, Chef Marcos arrives 2 to 2.5 hours prior to prep, cook, and set the table.

3. ARE TABLE SETTING & CLEANUP INCLUDED?

Yes, 100%! We provide full restaurant-style table setting, plated or family-style service, and thorough kitchen cleanup, leaving your counters, stove, and dishware sparkling clean.

4. CAN YOU ACCOMMODATE SPECIAL DIETS?

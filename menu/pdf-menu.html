FAQ & POLICIES

PREGUNTAS FRECUENTES & POLÍTICAS DE SERVICIO EN VILLAS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

HOW MANY OPTIONS DO WE HAVE?

Breakfast menu: pick 2 options

Lunch menu: pick 2 options

Dinner menu: pick 2 appetizers, 2 entrees, and 1 dessert

Special Menu: 2 appetizers, 1 entree, and 1 dessert

Kids menu: 1 option

HOW MUCH TIME DO YOU NEED TO PREPARE?

We will get to the villa 1 hour before the scheduled meal. 1:30 to 2:00 hours prior if the group is more than 12 guests.

WHEN DO WE PAY YOU?

We ask for a 30% deposit when making a reservation to secure your calendar dates, and the remainder upon service completion.

WHAT DOES THE BARTENDER SERVICE INCLUDE?

The bartender service includes mixing and serving handcrafted drinks. You can provide the alcohol and mixers, or we can purchase them and you reimburse us.

WHAT ARE YOUR CANCELLATION POLICIES?

• 1 month ahead: Full reimbursement

• 2 weeks ahead: 50% reimbursement

• 1 week ahead: No reimbursement

HOW DO WE PAY?

You can pay with credit card, cash (USD / Costa Rican Colones), Zelle, or bank wire transfer.

IS THE GRATUITY INCLUDED?

Gratuity is not included; we kindly ask that you give it directly to Chef Marcos and assistants for outstanding service (customary 15-20%).

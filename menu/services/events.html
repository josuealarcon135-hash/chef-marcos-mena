Celebrations & Private Gatherings

Crafted for Life’s Most Meaningful Moments

Renting a luxury villa in Costa Rica is about celebrating life together. Chef Marcos Mena coordinates seamless culinary experiences for groups of 6 to 50+ guests.

Choose from interactive live-action culinary stations that delight guests of all ages:

  • Gourmet Costa Rican Taco Fiesta: Hand-pressed corn tortillas, shredded slow-cooked beef, citrus pork carnitas, fresh pico de gallo, guacamole, and artisanal charred salsas.
  • Live Wood-Fired Pizza & Pasta Station: Hand-stretched dough, San Marzano tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella, and gourmet toppings fired fresh.
  • Pacific Sushi & Asian Fusion Bar: Fresh tuna rolls, spicy salmon crunch, California king crab, and delicate nigiri prepared live.
  • Open-Air BBQ & Seafood Feast: Jumbo lobster tails, Argentine chorizo, flank steaks, and roasted tropical vegetables over hot flames.

Multi-Course Elegance

An Unforgettable Gastronomic Journey

When the Costa Rican sun dips below the Pacific horizon, Chef Marcos Mena transforms your dining terrace into an intimate Michelin-inspired restaurant.

Our dinner experiences celebrate the world-famous bounty of Quepos and Marina Pez Vela. Savor wild yellowfin tuna tartare with avocado foam, whole crispy Pacific red snapper, jumbo garlic-butter lobster tails, and prime cut Angus steaks grilled to perfection.

To conclude, enjoy live culinary theater with Chef Marcos’ signature dessert flambé — such as golden Crêpe Suzette with caramelized citrus liqueur or decadent warm brownies with artisan vanilla bean ice cream.

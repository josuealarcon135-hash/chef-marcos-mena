Craft Cocktails & Wine

Tropical Libations by the Pool

Nothing compares to sipping an ice-cold, handcrafted tropical cocktail while watching toucans and monkeys in the jungle canopy from your private villa deck.

Our dedicated mixologists bring high-end cocktail bar culture directly to your bar counter. We source local organic fruits, freshly pressed sugar cane juice, fresh mint, local passion fruit (maracuyá), ginger infusions, and premium spirits to design a personalized signature cocktail menu for your stay.

Whether pairing wines with a multi-course dinner, serving chilled coconut water mocktails to kids, or keeping cold beer and sangria flowing during an afternoon pool party, we take care of all bar prep, glassware, ice, and post-service cleanup.

✓

Signature Tropical Cocktails

Passion fruit mojitos, smoked spicy jalapeño margaritas, Costa Rican Guaro sours, and piña coladas.

✓

Artisan Non-Alcoholic Mocktails

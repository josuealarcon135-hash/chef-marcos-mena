Effortless Arrival Experience

Skip the Supermarket Lines on Vacation

After a long flight into San José and the scenic drive down to Quepos/Manuel Antonio, the last thing you want is spending your first vacation afternoon navigating crowded grocery stores.

With Chef Marcos Mena’s villa pre-stocking service, send us your customized grocery checklist ahead of time. Our culinary team shops at the premier local farmers' markets (*feria del agricultor*), specialty butchers, and organic stores in Quepos to ensure your villa is completely outfitted prior to your check-in.

From chilled Imperial beers, crisp Sauvignon Blanc, and local tropical juices to organic snacks, coffee beans, artisan chips, salsa, guacamole, and kid-approved favorites, your kitchen will be stocked and organized cleanly.

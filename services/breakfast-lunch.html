Morning & Midday Indulgence

A Luxurious Start to Every Day in Costa Rica

Every vacation morning should be completely stress-free. Let Chef Marcos Mena handle early morning market visits, meal preparation, and table presentation while you relax by the infinity pool.

Whether you crave authentic Costa Rican heritage with traditional Gallo Pinto, ripe sweet plantains, farm eggs, and artisanal Turrialba cheese, or an American-style gourmet feast featuring golden brioche French toast, fluffy buttermilk pancakes, and crispy smoked bacon — our menus are tailored to your family’s morning rhythm.

For lunch, enjoy light, sun-kissed dining: freshly caught Pacific ceviche marinated in wild lime, grilled seafood skewers, wood-fired flatbreads, homemade guacamole, and crisp organic garden salads served poolside as you soak in the Manuel Antonio coastline.
